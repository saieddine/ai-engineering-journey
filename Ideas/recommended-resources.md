# Recommended Learning Resources

## Status

Future

---

## Overview

The assistant recommends external learning resources after answering questions.

Recommendations may include:

- YouTube videos
- Bilibili videos
- Books
- Research papers
- Interactive websites

---

## Example

Student asks:

"What is a Binary Tree?"

↓

AI explains the concept

↓

Recommended Resources

- YouTube lecture
- Bilibili explanation
- Practice exercises
- Suggested reading

## Long-Term Goal

Allow students to continue learning beyond the chatbot conversation.
