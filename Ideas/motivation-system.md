# Motivation System

## Status

Future

---

## Overview

Every study session should begin with a short motivational interaction.

Instead of opening directly into the chat, the student receives personalized encouragement based on previous progress.

---

## Examples

"Welcome back! Last week you completed 9 practice questions."

"You are only two lectures away from finishing this course."

"You've improved significantly in Binary Trees."

---

## Long-Term Goal

Increase consistency and reduce procrastination.

The AI should become both a tutor and a study coach.

---

## Future Improvements

- AI-generated motivational videos
- Daily quotes
- Progress badges
- Weekly goals
