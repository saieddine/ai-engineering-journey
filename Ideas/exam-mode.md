# Exam Preparation Mode

## Status

Planned

---

## Overview

A dedicated study mode activated during exam periods.

The assistant changes its behavior from teaching concepts to maximizing exam performance.

---

## Features

- Intensive revision plans
- Past exam analysis
- Frequently asked questions
- High-priority topics
- Practice quizzes
- Time management recommendations

---

## Goal

Help students prepare efficiently during the final days before examinations.
